Coupons with fibers aligned with the 0 degree direction align closest with the linear elasticity theory and give more linear response. Coupon 9 agrees especially well because it has 3 of 4 of its plies oriented at 0. But Coupon 1, with all 4 fibers in that direction, performs worse than expected, showing that the alignment alone does not account for the results, and this may be potentially due to a resin failure, and how one 90 degree ply of Coupon 9 might hold it transversely better. 

Asymmetric one, such as Coupons 3 and 5, have coupling between stretching and bending, making their response more sensitive to the geometry of the test setup and the method with which they are secured. Coupon 6, despite having ±45° fibers, performs exceptionally well, and its extension/bending coupling is not as present as in asymmetric coupons like Coupon 5.
